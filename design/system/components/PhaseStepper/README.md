# PhaseStepper

A project's route through its phases (default: our seven).

- **Done** phases are filled violet; the **current** one has a halo; **skipped** ones are dashed and struck through.
- **Flags** mark phases with a client sign-off gate.
- **`compact`** suits the client portal and cards.
- **Props:** `current`, `skipped`, `gates`, `phases`.
