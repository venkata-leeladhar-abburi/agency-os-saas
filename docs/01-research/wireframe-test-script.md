# Wireframe test script (round R1, after G1 wireframes)

Moderated, remote, 30 to 40 minutes, with the clickable wireframes at `/prototype` (run `pnpm dev` and open http://localhost:3000/prototype, or the Vercel preview once it is connected). Five agency owners in India, two freelancers, three clients (two on Android phones).

## Before the session
- Consent to record; say it's the product being tested, not them.
- Open the screen named in each task with the default state. Hide the prototype controls with `?chrome=0` in the address.

## Tasks

| # | Say to the participant | Start screen | Done when they… | Target |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| T1 | Sign up and set up your workspace with your logo and services. | S01 → S02 | reach Today with the checklist | 5 min |
| T2 | A new enquiry came from Instagram. Add it and set a follow-up for Friday. | S12 | find Add lead and the Follow-up field (S15) | 1 min |
| T3 | Send this lead a quote for a five-page website with GST. | S14 | open the quote builder and find Save and send (S18) | 4 min |
| T4 | The client accepted. Make them a client and invite them to the portal. | S14 (Mark as won) | reach the conversion dialog (S21) and keep the invite ticked | 1 min |
| T5 | Start the project using your usual process. | S24 | choose Seven phases and Create project (S25) | 3 min |
| T6 | Ask the client to approve the designs. | S26 | find Request approval (S27 dialog) | 1 min |
| T7 | (Client, phone) You got a link on WhatsApp. Find what needs you and approve the design. | S38 | tap the approval and Approve (S39) | 2 min |
| T8 | (Client, phone) Pay the advance. | S38 | reach Pay and choose a method (S40) | 1 min |
| T9 | Bill your US client US$2,000 for the first milestone as an export invoice. | S32 | choose Export under LUT and see the LUT line (S33) | 4 min |
| T10 | How much money is overdue, and which project is at risk? | S11 | point at Overdue and Projects at risk | 30 s |

After each task: "How easy was that, from 1 (very hard) to 7 (very easy)?" (SEQ).

## What to note
First click, wrong paths, hesitation over 5 seconds, words they didn't understand, and anything they expected to see. Log issues as blocker, major, minor or cosmetic in `synthesis.md`.

## Closing questions
- What would stop you using this for your next client?
- What did you expect to find that wasn't there?
