## Top insights so far (from desk research; confirm in interviews)

1. **Lead-to-payment in one flow is the baseline abroad** (HoneyBook, Dubsado, Bonsai, Plutio). We must match it before our differentiators matter.
2. **The leading client-flow tools don't serve India.** HoneyBook is limited to the US, Canada, UK and Australia, and none of those reviewed shows GST, LUT or UPI.
3. **Indian tools are accounting-first** (Refrens, Zoho Invoice), so delivery and the client experience are open.
4. **Agency operations tools charge per user in EUR or USD** and suit larger firms. A flat INR workspace price is a real difference.
5. **Directories bring leads but no workflow,** and their profiles show stars and price, not process.
6. **Handoff isn't sold as a feature** by anyone we reviewed. It's a likely edge for software agencies.
7. **AI assistants and MCP connectors are now common.** Plan AI drafts that are transparent and reviewable.
8. **Indian clients live in WhatsApp and pay by UPI.** The portal must open from a WhatsApp link and take UPI in two taps.
9. **Compliance shapes the design:** GST and LUT invoices, the DPDP Act and GDPR, and WCAG 2.1 AA (also the EU accessibility direction).
10. **The free tier is a trial for most competitors.** The deck's free-forever profile and tools can be our top of funnel, with clear limits.
