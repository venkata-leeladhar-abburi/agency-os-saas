# Interview guides

Four guides, 45 to 60 minutes each, remote (Google Meet) or in person. Ask about real past events ("Tell me about the last time…"), not opinions about features. Never pitch Agency OS during the discovery part. Show concepts only in the last 10 minutes, if at all.

**Before every interview:** consent read out and confirmed (recording, purpose, storage, right to stop). Note the participant ID (for example IN-AO-03), never the name, in the notes.

**Language:** English, Telugu or Hindi; let the participant choose.

---

## Guide A: agency owner or freelancer (India and international)

### 1. Warm-up (5 min)
- Tell me about your business: what you do, who you work with, how big the team is.
- Which kinds of clients do you work with: local, other states, other countries?

### 2. Finding work (8 min)
- Think of your last three clients. How did each one find you?
- What made them pick you over others? How do you know?
- Where do you show your work today? What would you like to show that you can't?
- *International probe:* How do foreign clients check that you're trustworthy?
- *India probe:* Do you use IndiaMART, Instagram, LinkedIn or directories? What works?

### 3. Leads to signed deal (10 min)
- Walk me through your last enquiry, from the first message to the signed deal. Which tools did you touch?
- Where do you keep track of leads? Show me if you can (screen share).
- How long does it take to send a quote or proposal? What do you reuse?
- Tell me about a deal you lost. Why do you think you lost it?
- How do you make contracts? How do clients sign?

### 4. Onboarding and delivery (10 min)
- After a client says yes, what happens in the first week?
- What do you collect from clients (logos, content, logins)? How?
- How do you plan a project? Is your process written down anywhere? Does it change per project?
- How does the client know what's happening? How often do they ask?
- Tell me about the last time a client asked for a change. What happened about the cost?
- How do clients approve designs or milestones today?
- *Team probe:* How do you know who's doing what and who is overloaded?

### 5. Money (8 min)
- Walk me through your last invoice: how you made it, sent it, and got paid.
- *India probe:* GST: are you registered? How do you handle GST on invoices? Any trouble?
- *Export probe:* For foreign clients: currency, LUT, FIRA, fees, how they pay?
- *International probe:* VAT, Stripe, card payments, payment terms?
- How often do you chase payments? How?
- Do you know which projects made money last quarter? How?

### 6. Finishing (5 min)
- What do you hand over at the end? How?
- Tell me about a problem that came up after handover.
- Do you ask for reviews or referrals? How?

### 7. Tools and money spent (4 min)
- Which tools do you pay for today, roughly how much a month?
- Have you tried an all-in-one tool? What happened?
- What would make you switch?

### 8. Concept reaction (optional, 5 min)
Show the Agency OS journey (10 stages) and the profile concept.
- Which three stages would help you most right now? Why?
- What would stop you from using it?
- *Price (survey-style):* At what monthly price would this feel too cheap to trust? A bargain? Getting expensive? Too expensive?

### 9. Wrap-up
- Anything I should have asked?
- Can we show you designs in a few weeks?

## Guide B: team member or project lead (India)

1. Warm-up: role, team, typical project.
2. Walk me through yesterday from start to finish. Which tools, which messages?
3. Where do requirements come from? Tell me about a time they changed late.
4. How do you post updates, mark attendance and log time today?
5. How do you get client approvals? Tell me about one that went wrong.
6. How are logins and credentials shared on a project?
7. What do you do at handover? What's always missing?
8. If one thing about your workday could change, what would it be?

## Guide C: client, Indian SMB owner

**Notes:** keep the language simple. Prefer Telugu or Hindi if they're more comfortable. Interview by phone or in person, at the client's pace.

1. Tell me about your business and the website, app or software you had built.
2. How did you choose the agency? What did you check?
3. How did you agree on the price and scope? Did you sign anything? How?
4. While it was being built, how did you know what was happening? How often did you ask?
5. How did you approve designs or screens? Tell me about one approval.
6. How did you pay? UPI, bank transfer, cheque? Did any payment cause confusion?
7. At the end, what did you receive? Was anything missing?
8. Which apps do you use every day on your phone? (Observe: WhatsApp, UPI apps, others.)
9. *Concept (optional):* Show the client portal home on a phone. Ask them to find what they need to do and what they owe. Observe; don't help.
10. Would you prefer this in Telugu, Hindi or English?

---

## Guide D: client abroad, founder or marketing lead

1. Tell me about the last time you hired an agency or freelancer, especially from another country.
2. How did you find and check them? What made you trust them?
3. What did you sign (NDA, contract, IP terms)? How?
4. How did you work across time zones? What worked and what didn't?
5. How did you track progress and approve work?
6. How did invoices and payments work (currency, method, fees)?
7. What did you get at handover? What was missing, and what happened next?
8. What would make you hire an Indian agency again, or never again?
9. *Concept (optional):* agency profile with process and toolkit; handoff page. Reactions?
