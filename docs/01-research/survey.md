# Screener and survey

One form, in two parts. Part A screens for interview candidates; part B is the survey for everyone who qualifies. Build it in Google Forms or Tally, around 8 minutes long. Target 150+ responses: about 100 from India and 50+ international.

**Consent line at the top:** "We're researching how agencies and freelancers run client work. Answers are used only for this research and stored securely. You can ask us to delete them at any time (privacy@…)."

## Part A: screener

1. Which best describes you?
   - Agency owner or founder
   - Freelancer working alone
   - Employee or project lead at an agency
   - I hire agencies or freelancers (client)
   - None of these *(end the survey)*
2. Where are you based? (country; if India, city)
3. Team size (agencies): 2 to 5 / 6 to 10 / 11 to 20 / 21 to 50 / 50+
4. Main services (pick up to 3): Branding / UI-UX design / Websites / Web or mobile apps / ERP or software / Marketing and ads / Video and content / Other
5. Where are your clients? Mostly in my city / Across India / Abroad / A mix of India and abroad
6. Would you join a 45-minute paid interview (₹1,000 or US$40 voucher)? Yes (email or phone) / No

## Part B: survey

### Current work
7. In the last 3 months, where did your new clients come from? (pick all) Referrals / Instagram / LinkedIn / Website / IndiaMART / Directories (Clutch, GoodFirms) / Freelance marketplaces / Ads / Other
8. How many new enquiries do you get in a typical month? 0 to 5 / 6 to 15 / 16 to 40 / 40+
9. How many projects run at the same time? 1 to 2 / 3 to 5 / 6 to 10 / 10+

### Pain by stage
10. How painful is each part of your work today? (1 = no problem, 5 = very painful)
    Getting found · Tracking leads · Quotes and proposals · Contracts and signing · Client onboarding · Planning projects · Managing the team's work · Client updates and approvals · Change requests · Invoicing and tax · Getting paid on time · Handing over · Getting reviews and referrals
11. Which ONE of these would you fix first? (same list)

### Tools and spend
12. Which tools do you use today? (pick all) WhatsApp / Gmail / Google Sheets / Notion / Trello / ClickUp / Asana / Jira / Zoho / Refrens / Tally / QuickBooks / Xero / HoneyBook / Dubsado / Bonsai / HubSpot / Canva / Figma / Other
13. Roughly how much do you spend on software for client work each month? ₹0 / under ₹1,000 / ₹1,000 to 3,000 / ₹3,000 to 8,000 / ₹8,000+ (international: matching USD bands)

### Money (India)
14. Are you registered for GST? Yes / No / Not sure
15. Do you invoice foreign clients? Never / Sometimes / Every month
16. How do clients usually pay you? (pick all) UPI / Bank transfer / Card / PayPal / Wise / Cheque / Cash
17. How often do you chase late payments? Rarely / Sometimes / Most invoices

### Clients and process
18. How do clients approve your work today? WhatsApp message / Email / Call / Tool / They don't, formally
19. Is your way of working (process, checklists, templates) written down? Yes, and reused / Partly / No
20. Would you show your process and tools on a public profile? Yes / Maybe / No. Why? (optional text)

### Price (Van Westendorp), for agency owners and freelancers
For a tool that handles leads, proposals, client portal, projects and invoices:
21. At what monthly price would it be so cheap you'd doubt its quality?
22. At what price would it be a bargain?
23. At what price would it start to feel expensive, but you'd still consider it?
24. At what price would it be too expensive to consider?

### Open
25. What's the most annoying part of running client work today? (text)

## Analysis plan

- Pain heat map by stage, split by segment (India agencies, India freelancers, international).
- Top-3 "fix first" stages per segment.
- Price curves per segment (agency, solo).
- Cross-tabs: foreign clients × invoicing pain; team size × team-management pain.
- Feeds assumptions A1, A2, A5, A10 and the build order.
