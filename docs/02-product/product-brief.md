# Agency OS: product brief

**A product by One Stop Solutions.**

## One line

Agency OS is one platform where freelancers and agencies get found, win clients, run projects and hand them over, with the Playbook (how you work, written down once) used everywhere.

## The problem

Work arrives in DMs. Delivery ends in zip files. In between, agencies juggle sheets, chat threads, documents, invoice tools and payment links. Clients keep asking "what's the status?" Changes go unbilled, payments come late, and handoffs miss logins and documents. Tools from abroad don't handle Indian tax and payments; Indian tools are built around accounting, not client delivery. (Evidence: `../01-research/`.)

## What it does: three layers on one Playbook

1. **Get found:** free public profile with portfolio, verified reviews and a "How we work" page built from the Playbook; marketplace by service and city; free tools.
2. **Grow:** leads pipeline with sources; quotes and proposals with e-signature; lead to client in one click.
3. **Deliver and run:** client portal; projects from your own phases with approval gates; tasks and team; documents; invoices and payments; handoff package.
4. **Playbook (under everything):** process, SOPs, prompts, tools, templates and learnings, reused at every stage.

Nine portals give each role its own view: public and marketplace, agency owner, hirer, solo, client, project lead, team member, HR and super admin. Full detail in `flows.md`.

## What makes it different

1. **Playbook:** your process saved once and reused on every project.
2. **Profiles that show how you work:** process and toolkit, not only stars and a price.
3. **Projects from your own phases,** with client approval gates.
4. **Portals for every role,** including project lead, team and HR.
5. **Document studio, handoff package and international client mode.**
6. **India-first money that also works abroad:** GST, export invoices under LUT, UPI links, multi-currency.
7. **Zero commission,** ever. Hirers never pay to browse or contact; listings never expire.

## Product principles

1. **Value in the first session:** a new workspace sends its first proposal or invoice within minutes.
2. **One record, no retyping:** lead becomes client becomes project becomes invoice becomes handoff, carrying data forward.
3. **The client experience sells the agency:** a branded, plain-language portal that works on a phone from a WhatsApp link.
4. **Show, don't ask:** progress, dues and "Needs you" are always visible, so nobody has to ask for status.
5. **India-first, world-ready:** INR, GST and UPI by default; currency, tax and time zones per client.
6. **Calm by design:** few colours, clear hierarchy, one primary action per screen. The UI/UX rulebook lives in `../03-design/ux-principles.md`.
7. **Ours by default:** zero commission; data export any time.

## Success (detail in `metrics.md`)

- **Activation:** first proposal or invoice sent within 7 days of sign-up.
- **North star:** paid invoices per active workspace per month (money flowing through Agency OS means it's running the business).
- **Retention:** month-3 workspace retention; weekly active owners.
- **Client experience:** share of clients who open the portal; approvals done in the portal.
